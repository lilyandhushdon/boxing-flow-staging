How it works

Three taps to the first bell

  1. 01

    Pick your focus

    Choose from 19 skill areas, from jab technique and footwork to body shots, feints and conditioning. Pick one or mix several.

  2. 02

    Choose your rounds

    From three to twelve rounds of three or five minutes, with a minute of rest between each.

  3. 03

    Follow the coach

    A five-second countdown, the bell, then a new drill every 30 seconds. The voice tells you what to throw and how to throw it.

In your corner

Built for the bag, the floor and the garage

Set your phone down, gloves on, and let the app run the clock.

3D coach

The timer flips over to a 3D coach who shows you every drill. Double-tap it to step into interactive mode and watch him work the ring from ringside in a full 3D gym.

Bells and countdown

A spinning five-second countdown and a "Fight!" burst before every round, bells at the start and end.

Music, mixed like a set

Workout tracks blend into each other with DJ-style crossfades, and dip whenever the coach speaks.

Crowd on your side

An arena crowd that roars on the bell and lifts in the final ten seconds, and never loops the same way twice.

Change it mid-round

Scroll everything that's coming up and drag drills into a new order without stopping the clock.

Nothing to install

Runs in the browser on your phone or laptop. No account and no sign-up. Open it and train.

More ways to train

Beyond the bag

Fight scenarios, punch-output rounds, strength and conditioning, and the mental side of boxing. Pick one and you are in.

Fight scenarios

Face a pressure fighter, a counterpuncher or an outfighter. A coach talks you through how to beat that style, one point at a time.

Output rounds

Pick a punch target, from 90 to 500 a round. The coach calls every combination and you throw it, until you hit the number.

Plyometrics

Explosive jumps, bounds, push-ups and medicine ball throws, with a different set of moves every round.

Shoulder conditioning

Build the endurance to keep your hands up through the last round, with a drill you can do at the end of any session.

Mental skills

Box breathing, reframing, self-talk and visualisation. Train the calm and focus behind your boxing.

Boxing IQ

Ring situations as they happen, and what to do about them. Work out the answer, then see why it works.
