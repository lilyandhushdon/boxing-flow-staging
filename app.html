Mental skills

Train your mind like you train your body. Short, regular practice is what works.

Box breathing

Breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold, all for the same count, tracing the sides of a box. Slow, even breathing can help settle nerves and sharpen your focus before a fight, between rounds or after a hard exchange. Breathe through your nose if you can, and go back to normal breathing if you feel dizzy.
